Privacy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Aeacus marks which parts of a piece of writing read as AI generated and which read as written by a person. It does that on your own device, and the text it looks at never leaves it.

What Aeacus reads

On this website, the text you paste into the box. In the browser extension, the text of the page you ask it to check: the paragraphs, reviews and comments in view at the moment you press the button or the shortcut. Nothing else on the page is read, and no page is read until you ask for that check.

Where that text goes

Nowhere. The model that scores it runs inside your browser or on your Mac, and no copy of the text is sent to a server. There is no account to make, no history of what you checked, and no analytics, advertising or crash reporting built into the site or the extension.

What Aeacus downloads

The model itself, about 90 MB, once. It comes from a public Hugging Face repository and is kept on your device afterwards, so a later check does not download it again. The request asks for those files and carries nothing about the page you were reading.

What is kept on your device

The downloaded model, and the few settings that go with it: whether checks may use the GPU, whether the log is verbose, and whether the model has finished downloading. Nothing there describes what you read or what the model said about it.

This website

The site is served by Cloudflare Pages and keeps standard access logs the way any web host does: the address requested, the time, and the browser's user agent. Those logs describe the page load, never the text you paste, which stays in your browser. There are no cookies beyond what serving the files requires.
